Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Cadena Legal Pty Ltd ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights under the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. Information we collect

We collect the information you provide when you use lawkitty, including your email address and name when you create an account or sign in, details of how you use the service such as searches and pages viewed, and anything you send us when you get in touch.

2. How we use it

We use your information to provide and improve the service, manage your account, respond to your requests, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies to keep you signed in and make the service work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers who help us run lawkitty, such as our authentication, payment and email providers. We share only what is necessary for each provider to do its job.

5. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, and to hold it securely.

6. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as we need it for billing, legal or other legitimate purposes, after which we delete or de-identify it.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: meow@lawkitty.app.
