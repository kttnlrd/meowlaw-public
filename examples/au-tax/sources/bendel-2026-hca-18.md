Commissioner of Taxation v Bendel [2026] HCA 18

AI-GENERATED SUMMARY: machine-generated, not the judgment text.

# Facts

The Steven Bendel 2005 Discretionary Trust was established by deed, with Gleewin Pty Ltd as trustee. In each year from 2014 to 2017, Gleewin resolved to set aside all net income of the trust for beneficiaries including Gleewin Investments Pty Ltd (a private company) and Mr Bendel. The amounts set aside were not paid to Gleewin Investments but were retained by Gleewin, and Gleewin Investments never called for payment. The Commissioner issued amended assessments treating these unpaid present entitlements as loans under s 109D of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1936 (Cth), deeming dividends to have been paid. The Administrative Appeals Tribunal and Full Federal Court both rejected the Commissioner's contentions.

# Issues

- Whether the resolutions to set aside net income effected a distribution or merely created separate trusts.
- Whether the unpaid present entitlements were held on separate trusts with sufficient certainty of subject matter.
- Whether a debtor/creditor relationship arose between the trustee and the corporate beneficiary.
- Whether the unpaid present entitlements fell within the expanded definition of 'loan' in s 109D(3) of the ITAA 1936, particularly as a provision of credit or financial accommodation or a transaction in substance effecting a loan.
- Whether the Commissioner's approach resulted in double taxation contrary to s 6-25 of the ITAA 1997.

# Held

By a 5:2 majority, the High Court dismissed the Commissioner's appeal (Gageler CJ, Gordon, Edelman, Steward and Gleeson JJ in the majority; Jagot J and Beech-Jones J dissenting). The majority held that the trustee's resolutions did not create an unconditional duty to pay; they created separate trusts over the set-aside amounts, so no debtor/creditor relationship arose and no loan was made to the corporate beneficiary for the purposes of s 109D of the ITAA 1936. Jagot J (with whom Beech-Jones J agreed) dissented, holding that s 109D(1) applied to the loans which Gleewin Investments made to Gleewin in the relevant years.
