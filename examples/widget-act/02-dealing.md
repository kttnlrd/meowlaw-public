# Part 2 Dealing in widgets

## Section 3 Offence

(1) A dealer must not sell a widget except as permitted by section 4 or sections 5 to 6.

(2) Subsection (1) does not apply to a widget given as a gift.

## Section 4 Permitted sales

A dealer may sell a widget at the retail price if the dealer is entered on the register.

## Section 5 Meaning of dealer

In this section, "trade" means a business carried on for profit.

A dealer is a person who sells widgets in trade.

## Section 6 Penalty

A person who contravenes section 3 commits an offence. See also Article 4 of the Model Code.

# Part 3 Administration

## Section 7 Register

The Registrar must keep a register of dealers.
