# Staff handbook

This handbook applies to Example Pty Ltd (the "Company") and its employees (each an "Employee").

## Selling widgets

An Employee must not sell a widget unless the Company is entered on the register. See section 4 of the Widget Act 2024 and Smith v Jones [2024] EXSC 1.

## Gifts

Widgets given to customers as gifts are covered by section 3(2) of the Widget Act 2024.
